## 2. How it was tested

- **A separate copy of the app:** API on `localhost:8001` and website on `localhost:5174` (`backend-audit` and `frontend-audit` in `.claude/launch.json`), using a freshly seeded `opendoor_verify` database, with mail written to `storage/logs/laravel.log` instead of sent. The `opendoor` database and the servers on 8000/5173 were not touched.
- **Roles walked through:** a guest; a newly registered job seeker; `candidate@`; `employer@` (owner); `hr@`; a new HR officer who joined from an invite link; a newly registered company owner; `admin@`.
- **Rules probed directly on the API:** duplicate applications, applying to closed or draft jobs, unverified email, HR scope, owner-only actions, a suspended user's session, used invite links, categories in use, rate limits.
- **Phone width:** every main page at 360 px (no page scrolls sideways).
- **Display settings:** text size slider (100–200 %), high contrast, quiet mode, compact menu, reduced motion, saved across reloads.

## 4. Problems found

Ranked by what matters most before the audit (Oct 18) and the usability tests (Oct 19–20). Each one was seen in the running app or confirmed in the code.

| # | Severity | Problem | Where | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Medium | **There is no way to contact the team.** The accessibility statement's "Report a problem" says "please tell the OpenDoor team" with no address or link (F13.5 asks for "how to report barriers"). The suspended-account message says "Contact the OpenDoor administrators", also with no way to do it. The footer has no **Contact us** link, although revamp §1.2 says it should | `AccessibilityPage.jsx:21-24`, `SiteFooter.jsx`, the login suspension message | Add one team email (a `mailto:` link) to the statement and to a **Contact us** footer link |
| P2 | Medium | **The Privacy and Accessibility pages still say "Draft for review by the OpenDoor project team."** Participants and the audit will read that line. The statement also doesn't mention the new display settings (revamp §5 says it should) | `InfoPage.jsx:14`, `AccessibilityPage.jsx` | Have the team approve the text, remove the draft line, and add a sentence on Text size, High contrast, Quiet mode and Reduce motion |
| P3 | Medium | **Logged-in users get the guest header on public pages.** On Home, `/jobs/:id` and `/employers/:id`, a job seeker or employer sees Home · Find jobs · "Go to my dashboard", with no Notifications, Account or Log out, and that "Find jobs" opens the unranked public board. The revamp plan says each role has one header on every page | `PublicLayout` / `SiteHeader` for logged-in users | Show the role's header (`portalMenus`) when someone is logged in, on public pages too |
| P4 | Low | **Rate-limit errors read "Too Many Attempts."** (Laravel's text) on Register (10 a minute per IP), Change password, Apply, Feedback and the invite pages. Only "Send the link again" has its own message | `api/client.js` (`generalErrorFrom`) | Map status 429 to a plain sentence such as "Too many tries. Please wait a minute and try again." |
| P5 | Low | **Double full stop:** after joining from an invite, the announcement reads "Welcome to OpenDoor Demo Corp.. You're now an HR officer in IT." | `JoinPage.jsx:122` | Use the `sentence()` guard from `notificationText.js`, as `MyApplicationsPage.jsx:19` already does |
| P6 | Low | **Wording about reports, which aren't built:** the Privacy notice says administrators "handle reports", and the Suspend dialog's hint gives the example "Posted fake jobs, reported by 3 job seekers" | `PrivacyPage.jsx:29`, `UserPage.jsx:258` | Drop "and handle reports"; use another example |
| P7 | Low | **After an "unconfirmed email" refusal, Apply has no Send button.** If the person confirms in another tab and comes back, they have to reload the page and lose the cover note they typed. The banner also stays until a reload | `ApplyPage.jsx:178` (`!refusal`) | For the `unverified` refusal, keep the button, or re-read `/me` when the window regains focus |
| P8 | Low | **The home page's tab title and heading differ:** the tab reads "Find jobs that meet your accommodation needs", the H1 "Find work that fits how you work" (uncommitted revamp) | `HomeHero.jsx:36` | Use the same words for both |
| P9 | Low | **Reopen asks for a new closing date even when the old one is still in the future** (the field starts empty) | Reopen dialog, `JobPostingsPage` | Fill in the current date when it's still valid |
| P10 | Low | **Previous on a page past the end** (`/jobs?page=5`) goes to page 4, which is also empty. Known Phase 3 leftover | `Pagination` | Point Previous at the last page |
| P11 | Low | **The hero mascot slides in from 120 px to the right**, and the hero doesn't clip it, so on a phone the page may scroll sideways for about a second while it settles. (Seen only as a paused frame in the hidden test browser; worth checking on a real phone.) | `HomeHero.jsx:113`, `--animate-hero-mascot` | Add `overflow-x: clip` to the hero section |
| P12 | Note | "Your top matches" on the job seeker dashboard can list jobs already applied to (*HR Assistant*, rejected, for `candidate@`) | Candidate dashboard | Leave out jobs with an application, if wanted |
| P13 | Note | The build is one 726 kB chunk. Revamp U2 says it should grow by less than 60 kB; no baseline was measured | `vite build` | Lazy-load the admin and employer portals if U2 matters |

**Checked and not a problem:**
- **Integration checker M1** (a suspended user stays "logged in" for one request): not reproduced. Suspending ends the person's sessions, so their next request gets 401 and the app logs them out.
- **HR officers** get 403 on other departments' postings, departments, invites, members, the company profile and verification. A profile update with missing fields answers 422 before the 403; nothing leaks.
- **Dialogs that seemed to stay open** during testing were caused by the hidden browser pane pausing animations, not by the app.
